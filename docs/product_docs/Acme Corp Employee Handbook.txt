ACME CORPORATION
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (2026 EDITION)

1. INTRODUCTION
Welcome to Acme Corporation. This handbook outlines policies, expectations, and employee benefits applicable to all full-time and contract employees.

2. WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Employees are expected to maintain core availability between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM.

3. REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week unless otherwise specified by their manager. Fully remote roles require quarterly in-person attendance.

4. LEAVE POLICY
- Annual Leave: 20 days per year
- Sick Leave: 10 days per year
- Bereavement Leave: 5 days
Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to 5 days.

5. PERFORMANCE REVIEW
Performance reviews are conducted bi-annually. Ratings range from:
- Exceeds Expectations
- Meets Expectations
- Needs Improvement

6. DATA SECURITY
Employees must not store sensitive company data on personal devices. All data access must go through approved corporate systems.

7. CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees must maintain professional behavior at all times. Harassment or violation of compliance policies will result in disciplinary action.

8. EXIT POLICY
Employees must provide a minimum of 2 weeks notice before resignation.
